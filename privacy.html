Beta privacy policy

Last updated 14 October 2026

This policy covers the ReelLove test version (TestFlight beta). It explains what we collect, why, and how to delete it.

Who we are

ReelLove is a dating app for people aged 18 and over in London. During this test phase it is run by Toby Butterworth, the data controller. ReelLove Ltd will take over once it is set up, and this policy will be updated then.

This is a test version shared through Apple's TestFlight. It may change, and test data may be deleted when testing ends. Questions or requests: hello@reellove.uk.

How we use it

Working out your taste. When you share a Reel, we read its public caption (including any hashtags) and send it to Claude, an AI model made by Anthropic, which picks out themes, vibes and a few specific topics in our own words. For TikTok, our server fetches the caption from TikTok. For Instagram, your phone reads the Reel’s link preview (the same preview iMessage shows) and sends us only the caption, not the account name or picture. The caption and hashtags are not stored; only our own themes, vibes and topics are kept.

Events. We may use your topics to invite you, in the app, to ReelLove events you’d enjoy, such as a jazz night for people who share jazz Reels.

Matching. Every day we compare people's taste profiles, preferences and distance. Claude then helps choose matches and writes a one-line reason for each. Claude only sees themes, vibes and a short summary of past choices. It never sees names, ages, photos or chats.

What other people see. A match sees your first name, age, photos, postcode area and rough distance, what you're looking for, the Reels you pin to your profile (up to 3), up to 3 more of your shared Reels picked for what you have in common (you can hide any), and the reason you were matched. Your taste profile is private: only you can see it.

Chats. Only you and your match can read your chat. If someone reports a chat, the ReelLove team can read the most recent messages in it to deal with the report.

Our legal bases (UK GDPR): providing the service you signed up for, your consent (for analysing the Reels you share, which you can withdraw by deleting them), and our legitimate interest in keeping the app safe.

We don't sell your data or show you ads.

Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your data while you have an account. You can delete it yourself at any time:

  • Delete my Reels and taste (Settings): removes every Reel you've shared and resets your taste profile.
  • Delete my account (Settings): permanently deletes your profile, photos, Reels, taste, matches and chats.

Reports about safety may be kept for a short time after an account is deleted, where needed to protect others. Test data may be deleted when the beta ends.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask to see, correct, delete or move your data, and object to how we use it. Email hello@reellove.uk and we'll reply within one month. If you're unhappy with our answer, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

ReelLove is for people aged 18 and over. We delete any account found to belong to someone younger.

We'll update this policy as the app changes, and tell you in the app about any important changes.
